## Research

**Interests:** Dispersive PDEs, Random dynamical system, Ergodicity and mixing, Large deviation principle

My research focuses on mixing and large deviations for randomly forced dispersive PDEs, combining techniques from PDE analysis, control theory, stochastic processes, and dynamical systems. In the absence of parabolic regularization, a central theme is the asymptotic compactness, which links nonlinear smoothing from dispersive dynamics to control properties and long-time statistics.
